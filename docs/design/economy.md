# Economy

The startup loan from The Big Space Corporation, how it gets repaid, and how a run ends. Money is in credits (cr). Time is in game hours; one hour is 2 seconds of real time.

## How a run ends

*Decided*

### Collapse

Oxygen, water or energy run out. The crew evacuates on the arrival ship and sends an emergency beacon, and the game is over. The score is the hours the platform operated.

### Sale

You can sell the station at any time. The score is the hours operated and the sale price, shown side by side.

**Proposed:** The sale price is the platform's value (modules, robots, stock at current prices, and recent profit) minus what's still owed on the loan, times your share of ownership.

## Startup loan

*Proposed · Starting numbers for balancing*

### What The Big Space Corporation provides

- **Arrival ship, module kits, 20 robots, 4 mining ships:** 113,000 cr
- **Operating cash for the first payrolls:** 12,000 cr
- **Loan:** 125,000 cr
- **Flat fee, 20%, no interest:** 25,000 cr
- **Total to repay:** 150,000 cr

A flat fee keeps the balance easy to read: every payment takes it straight down, with no interest growing on it.

### Payment schedule

A payment is due every 250 hours, about 8 minutes of real time. The minimum steps up as the platform grows. Anything above the minimum is your choice.

- **0–500 h: grace period, while there's no trade port:** 0 cr
- **500–1,500 h:** 1,000 cr
- **1,500–3,000 h:** 2,000 cr
- **3,000 h on:** 4,000 cr

How long the loan takes, depending on how much of each period's profit above the minimum goes to it. Modeled with the assumptions further down.

| Payment style | Owed at 2,000 h | Owed at 4,000 h | Owed at 6,000 h | Paid off at | Real time | Cash on hand then |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum only | 142,000 | 118,000 | 86,000 | 11,500 h | 6.4 h | ≈ 320,000 cr |
| 40% of extra profit | 140,000 | 103,000 | 46,000 | 7,500 h | 4.2 h | ≈ 98,000 cr |
| 70% of extra profit | 138,000 | 91,000 | 16,000 | 6,500 h | 3.6 h | ≈ 42,000 cr |

A good run that pays off the loan takes 2 to 4 sittings. Paying fast leaves little cash for entertainment, robots and repairs, so morale and production can suffer, which is the trade-off you described.

## Missed payments

*Proposed*

**Decided:** Missing a payment means surrendering a share of ownership to The Big Space Corporation. That share acts like a second mortgage, paid off after the startup loan.

**Proposed:** Each missed minimum payment gives up 3% of the platform. After the loan is paid off, buying shares back continues on the 4,000 cr schedule, priced at 3% of the platform's value at the time. Any share still held comes out of the sale price.

## Assumptions behind the numbers

*Proposed · To be tuned in play*

### Crew costs

- **Salary per human:** 1 cr / hour
- **Food and entertainment per human, average:** 0.5 cr / hour
- **Profit share, split among the crew:** 5% of each sale

### Growth on a good run

- **First 500 h: building, nothing sold:** 4 ships, 12 crew
- **By 3,000 h: ≈ 2,900 cr profit per 100 h:** ≈ 8 ships, 24 crew
- **By 6,000 h: ≈ 5,600 cr profit per 100 h:** ≈ 12 ships, 36 crew

Profit here is sales minus crew costs, before loan payments. Rising prices aren't counted, so a real run does a little better.
