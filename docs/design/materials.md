# Raw materials

Everything the mining ships bring home: what it's for on the platform and what it's worth to the freighter. The colored dot is the material's color in the game: on tanks, bins, cargo crates and the HUD.

## Nebula gases

*Ships carry up to 3 gas types per trip*

### Hydrogen

*H · In the game*

- **Found:** Very common, most of any nebula
- **Platform use:** Fusion fuel: all energy for the platform, ships and robots. Running out ends the game.
- **Goes to:** Gas storage, then the fusion generators
- **Sale value:** Low, plentiful
- **Real world:** About 90% of the atoms in a nebula.

### Helium

*He · In the game*

- **Found:** Common
- **Platform use:** Coolant for fusion generators and cold storage tanks; keeps fuel lines pressurized.
- **Goes to:** Gas storage
- **Sale value:** Low to medium
- **Real world:** Second most common element; inert and very cold as a liquid.

### Nitrogen

*N · In the game*

- **Found:** Uncommon
- **Platform use:** Breathable air needs a buffer gas, not pure oxygen; topped up as habitats leak. Fertilizer for habitat gardens.
- **Goes to:** Gas storage, then the electrolysis plant, which mixes it with oxygen into air
- **Sale value:** Low
- **Real world:** 78% of Earth's air. In nebulae it's mostly in molecules like ammonia.

### Helium-3

*³He · In the game*

- **Found:** Very rare, a lucky find mixed in with helium
- **Platform use:** Sold to the freighter. The most valuable thing the platform produces.
- **Goes to:** Its own helium-3 storage module, held until a freighter buys it
- **Sale value:** Very high, the jackpot gas
- **Real world:** A rare helium isotope, long proposed as a clean fusion fuel.

### Deuterium

*D · In the game*

- **Found:** Rare, mixed in with hydrogen
- **Platform use:** Blended into hydrogen for a hotter, more efficient fusion burn. Stretches fuel further.
- **Goes to:** Straight from the dock to the fusion generators. **Optional:** makes them more efficient, but they run without it.
- **Sale value:** Medium to high
- **Real world:** "Heavy hydrogen," about 1 in 6,400 hydrogen atoms in Earth's oceans.

### Xenon

*Xe · In the game*

- **Found:** Very rare
- **Platform use:** Ion-engine boost: ships topped up with xenon make faster round trips.
- **Goes to:** Straight from the dock to the ship dock's fuel supply. **Optional:** ships fly without it, just slower.
- **Sale value:** High
- **Real world:** Real spacecraft ion thrusters run on it; scarce and costly on Earth.

### Argon

*Ar · In the game*

- **Found:** Uncommon
- **Platform use:** Shielding gas for the refinery and for welding repairs. Refining magnesium and aluminum could require it.
- **Goes to:** Straight from the dock to the refinery. Found only in small amounts.
- **Sale value:** Low to medium
- **Real world:** Industry's standard welding gas; keeps hot metal from reacting.

**Decided:** Magnesium and aluminum can't be refined without argon; each batch uses a little. Iron, nickel, silicon, platinum and palladium refine without it.

### Neon

*Ne · In the game*

- **Found:** Uncommon
- **Platform use:** Lasers built at assembly: cutting tools, turret targeting, and long-range sensor upgrades that tell pirates from freighters more reliably.
- **Goes to:** Straight from the dock to assembly
- **Sale value:** Medium
- **Real world:** Roughly the fifth most common element in the universe; used in lasers.

### Methane

*CH₄ · In the game*

- **Found:** Uncommon, in the cold, dense parts of the nebula
- **Platform use:** The platform's only carbon source, used at assembly: plastics, carbon composites for lighter frames, habitat furnishings.
- **Goes to:** Straight from the dock to assembly
- **Sale value:** Low
- **Real world:** Common in cold molecular clouds, along with carbon monoxide.

**Why no oxygen gas?** Free oxygen is scarce in nebulae; it's mostly locked up in water and carbon monoxide. That fits the design: oxygen comes from comet ice through the electrolysis plant.

## Asteroid metals

*Ships carry up to 2 metal types per trip · refined into building materials*

### Iron

*Fe · In the game*

- **Found:** Very common
- **Platform use:** Steel: frame pieces, module shells, ship hulls. The backbone of every build.
- **Sale value:** Low
- **Real world:** Metallic asteroids are mostly iron.

### Nickel

*Ni · In the game*

- **Found:** Common, usually with iron
- **Platform use:** Alloyed with iron for tougher steel: armor plating, turret parts, generator housings.
- **Sale value:** Low to medium
- **Real world:** Iron-nickel is the classic asteroid metal.

### Magnesium

*Mg · In the game*

- **Found:** Common, in stony asteroids
- **Platform use:** Light alloys for mining ships and robots: lighter means faster and cheaper to run.
- **Sale value:** Low
- **Real world:** Light and strong, but burns when hot, so it's refined under a shielding gas.

### Silicon

*Si · In the game*

- **Found:** Very common, in stony asteroids
- **Platform use:** Chips for robots, sensors and the command computer. Glass for windows and the garden dome.
- **Sale value:** Low to medium
- **Real world:** Technically not a metal but a metalloid; stony asteroids are mostly silicate rock.

### Aluminum

*Al · In the game*

- **Found:** Common
- **Platform use:** Lightweight hull panels, wiring, habitat interiors.
- **Sale value:** Low to medium
- **Real world:** Light, conducts electricity well, and doesn't rust.

### Platinum

*Pt · In the game*

- **Found:** Rare
- **Platform use:** Catalysts: the electrolysis plant that splits water into oxygen. Sensor electronics.
- **Sale value:** Very high
- **Real world:** One of the main reasons people dream of asteroid mining.

### Palladium

*Pd · In the game*

- **Found:** Rare
- **Platform use:** Hydrogen filters that purify and store fusion fuel. Electronics.
- **Sale value:** Very high
- **Real world:** Can soak up about 900 times its own volume of hydrogen.

## Comet ice

*Goes straight from the dock to the electrolysis plant*

### Ice

*H₂O · In the game*

- **Found:** Only on comets passing by
- **Platform use:** The electrolysis plant turns it into water and oxygen, with a little hydrogen left over.
- **Sale value:** Not sold; kept for the crew
- **Real world:** Comet ice is "dirty": it also holds frozen carbon dioxide, ammonia and methane.

Idea: dirty ice bonus

Since real comets carry frozen gases, a comet run could sometimes bring back a small bonus of nitrogen or methane along with the ice. It would make comet trips worth a little more and tie comets into the gas economy.

## Units, storage and prices

*Proposed · Starting numbers for balancing; all of them are meant to be tuned in play*

### How units work

Everything is counted in units (u). A healthy mining ship carries 20 u per trip, split across the materials it's assigned. A worn ship carries less.

Rare materials are capped per trip, and the rest of the hold fills with the other materials on the assignment. A helium-3 run brings back at most 2 u of helium-3, so pairing it with helium fills the hold.

Gases that go straight to a module (deuterium 4 u, neon 3 u, xenon 2 u, argon 1 u, methane 5 u per trip at most) aren't stored or sold.

Each storage module is a set of containers, one per material. Pirates steal whole containers, contents and all.

### What a trip is worth

At base prices:

- **Nebula: hydrogen, helium, nitrogen:** ≈ 190 cr
- **Nebula: helium-3 and helium:** ≈ 890 cr
- **Asteroids: iron and nickel:** ≈ 320 cr
- **Asteroids: platinum and nickel:** ≈ 880 cr

Rare runs pay about three times as much. But asteroid runs wear ships fastest, and comet runs earn nothing directly.

### Galactic market

Each price wanders up to about 25% above or below its trend over a few hundred hours, a few minutes of real time.

The trend rises about 5% every 1,000 hours. Freighter supplies rise a little faster, about 6%, so margins tighten slowly over a long run.

Now and then a demand event moves one material hard for a while, such as "Helium-3 demand surge: +40% for 200 hours".

Refined metal sold through the trade port fetches 1.5 times the raw price.

### When freighters come

**Decided:** The commander calls the freighter. It won't come unless the platform has the minimum amount to trade, except when it's bringing crew you requested.

**Proposed:** The minimum is sellable stock worth 3,000 cr at current prices. Counting value rather than units lets a full helium-3 sphere qualify on its own. Since you choose when to call, you can wait for good prices, at the risk of storage filling up or pirates getting there first.

**Decided:** The freighter needs a trade port to dock. Raids start after the first freighter, so building the trade port also starts the pirate clock.

**Proposed:** After you call, the freighter arrives 40–60 hours later and shows up first on long-range sensors as an unknown contact. It buys at the prices on the day it docks.

**Decided:** Supplies are picked from what the freighter has on board once it docks: food and entertainment, plus spare parts, robots and materials the platform can't mine yet.
